# DispatchOPS v35.26 — V8 UI Final Polish

Applied on top of V7.

## Changes
- Bulk Organizer desktop layout now reserves ~27% for Today's Schedule and ~73% for Fleet + Scheduled Customers.
- Today's Schedule is a single Liquid Glass surface; alerts, KPI strip and actions are transparent/flat content with separators, not nested glass cards.
- Dashboard KPI and Availability detail dialogs/table surfaces use the same Liquid Glass material as the new app: blur, saturation, translucent controls, glass borders, highlight and depth.
- Detail tables have glass header/body rows and no legacy opaque table surface.
- Completion checkmarks for fully loaded invoices, customers and stores are circular, constrained, and the SVG check is explicitly sized inside the circle.
